From Cebu City

Public V-Hire

Option A:

  • Terminals: Go to the PUV Terminal at IT Park.
  • Route: Board a Tuburan-bound or Asturias-bound van traveling via the Cebu Transcentral Highway (TCH).
  • Drop-off: Alight at the Asturias Municipal Hall or ask the driver to drop you off directly at the highway junction in Barangay Owak.
  • Travel Time & Fare: Approx. 2 hours | ₱180–₱200 per seat.

Option B: Private Vehicle

  • Navigate via the Cebu Transcentral Highway (TCH) passing through Busay and Balamban. Once you hit the coast and cross into Barangay Owak, Asturias, turn left at the resort's signage into Sitio San Juan. Free parking is available on-site.

From Balamban

Option A: By Private Vehicle / Motorcycle

  • Head north along the Toledo-Tabuelan-San Remigio Road (West Coast Road).
  • Right after crossing the border into Barangay Owak, Asturias, look for the Asturias Beach House signage on the left side of the road.

Option B: Public Commute

  • Hail any northbound jeepney, bus (heading to Tuburan/San Remigio), or tricycle from the Balamban public terminal or national highway.
  • Ask to be dropped off at the Asturias Beach House junction in Brgy. Owak. From there, grab a quick tricycle or habal-habal (motorcycle taxi) straight to the beach beachfront.
